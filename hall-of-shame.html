Verify any provider yourself (real, public sources)

This is the legitimate basis for a watchlist — check official records directly:

  • FSMB DocInfo — physician (MD/DO) license status & board actions, all 50 states.
  • AADB — directory of state dental boards; each board posts disciplinary actions.
  • NPI Registry — confirm a provider's identity, specialty, and practice location.
  • Your state dental board "license lookup / disciplinary actions" page (search "[your state] dental board license verification").
