GLP-1 Prescribing for Sleep Apnea

Tirzepatide (Zepbound) is the first FDA-approved drug for moderate-to-severe OSA in adults with obesity (Dec 2024; BMI ≥ 30). In the SURMOUNT-OSA trial it cut AHI 55–63%, with ~43% reaching effective resolution — benefit is largely weight-mediated.

Important — channels, not a verified prescriber list. Named individual prescribers can't be reliably enumerated. In practice, primary care writes ~84% of new tirzepatide scripts and sleep medicine ~0.2% — so the realistic path is an obesity-medicine specialist, your PCP, or a telehealth platform. Eligibility, pricing, and OSA-specific workflows change frequently; confirm directly. Not medical advice.
